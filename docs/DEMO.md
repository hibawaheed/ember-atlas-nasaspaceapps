# A three-minute demonstration

## Easiest first visit

Open the live app and choose **Show me how** or **Open guided lab** in the sidebar. Each of five chapters loads its own conditions and explains the next action. You can still move the sliders yourself. The v1.2 workspace uses a dark instrument theme; the exported HTML brief stays light for printing.

- **Play fire / Pause fire** controls the moving flame. **0.5× / 1× / 2×** changes animation speed, not experimental time.
- **Measured replay** uses real published spread-rate values. **Play observations** advances the listed points; its timing is a presentation cadence, not recorded test time.
- **Mission desk** asks where the research will be used and which question matters. Select up to three investigations to compare their experimental context.
- **How to use & learn** contains controls, science explainers, a glossary, and a knowledge check.

## Presentation script

1. **The problem (20 s):** A flame's appearance and behavior change in reduced gravity. The research exists, but experiment conditions and findings are fragmented. EMBER makes that evidence explorable.
2. **Explore (40 s):** Open the combustion lab. Drag the 3D flame. Compare with Earth, then load the oxygen-enriched habitat. Explain that the renderer is illustrative and that oxygen partial pressure is calculated separately from the visual activity index.
3. **Interpret (30 s):** Click Interpret scenario. Follow the related findings and their limitations. The ranking is relevance, not a risk score.
4. **Ground it (40 s):** Switch to Measured replay. Show M7/M8, explain that radius/brightness encode spread rate, then open Measured data to inspect the 39 published observations from 18 runs and changing oxygen conditions.
5. **Ask (30 s):** Ask “Why can a flame survive without airflow?” Show the NASA citations and the distinction between an observation and an inference. The default mode is transparent evidence retrieval; generative synthesis is optional.
6. **Take it forward (20 s):** Show the Mission desk: choose a lunar base and Detection, inspect the ranking reasons, then compare SAME and FLEX. Export a research brief or shortlist and show the original PSI links.

No claimed safety thresholds, mission certification, or NASA affiliation. The strongest story is the connection between evidence, context, and an honest interactive explanation.
